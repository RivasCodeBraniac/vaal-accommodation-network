Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our student accommodation.

Do you accept NSFAS applicants?

Yes. NSFAS applicants are welcome to apply, subject to the applicable accommodation requirements.

Do you accept private bursary students?

Yes. Students funded through private bursaries are welcome to apply.

Is there a deposit fee?

Some accommodations require a deposit fee, while some do not. Please check the details of your preferred accommodation.

Can I view the accommodation?

Yes. Contact us on WhatsApp to arrange a suitable viewing time.

Can I apply without viewing?

Yes. You can apply directly through our online application form.

What documents are required?

Required documents must be provided before moving into the accommodation. Specific documents may vary depending on the property.

Are the rooms furnished?

Yes. All our rooms are fully furnished for comfortable student living.

Still Have Questions?

Contact us directly and we will be happy to assist you.
