How to Apply for Accommodation

Choose the right property before submitting your application.

1

Choose Your Institution & Funding

2

Select Your Accommodation

Compare properties and click Apply for the exact accommodation you want.

3

Submit Your Application

Complete the form with your details. Our team will contact you on WhatsApp regarding the next steps.
